PROJECT / 01

GPS-Denied Navigation & Sensor Fusion

Master's thesis research · Carleton University · 2024–2026

Published journal article ↗
0.26 mPosition RMSE on a difficult recorded drone-flight sequence, versus 0.33 m for a quaternion UKF benchmark.

The challenge

Estimate position and orientation when GPS is unavailable, while managing drift from inertial sensors and uncertainty in visual measurements.

What I developed

A dual quaternion-based Unscented Kalman Filter using a six-dimensional local pose-error representation and tightly coupled stereo visual-inertial measurements. Tested on three recorded EuRoC drone-flight sequences and compared with benchmark filters.

Research visuals / Visual-inertial navigation

DQUKF architecture, trajectory-estimation performance, and visual-inertial processing using the EuRoC MAV dataset.

PROJECT / 02

Robotic Drilling & Joint Control

Research internship · KFUPM · Summer 2023

IEEE conference paper ↗
98% / 94%Reductions in simulated end-effector position-tracking RMSE along the X / Y axes using computed-torque control compared with PID.

The challenge

Design a robotic drilling model that can follow a target end-effector trajectory while accounting for coupled joint dynamics and the torques required by the task.

What I developed

A 3-DOF articulated-arm model in SolidWorks and MATLAB/Simulink. Derived the robot's kinematics and dynamics, planned trajectories, and evaluated PID and computed-torque control using simulation tracking-error metrics.

Design / Analysis / Simulation

Explore the robot design, kinematic model, Simulink control architecture, and simulated motion.

PROJECT / 03

Constrained Model Predictive Control

Graduate course project · Carleton University · 2024–2025

20Prediction steps
5Control steps
50 msSampling period

The challenge

Stabilize an inherently unstable inverted pendulum while accounting for actuator-force and state constraints, and examine the effects of disturbances and model mismatch.

What I developed

A constrained MPC formulation tested with linearized and nonlinear pendulum dynamics. In the reported setup, the actuator-force range was relaxed from ±10 N to ±15 N to make the specified state-constrained problem feasible. The project also studied LQR behavior and terminal-set constraints.

Control engineering / MATLAB simulation

From modeling the unstable cart–pendulum system to comparing prediction horizons and controller input behavior.

PROJECT / 04

Automated Water Filtration System

Senior capstone · KFUPM · 2023

3Filtration stages
4Sensor types
1Integrated prototype

The challenge

Build an automated water-treatment prototype combining a mechanical fluid circuit with sensor-driven operation and fault handling.

What I developed

An Arduino Mega-based system integrating pressure, temperature, flow, and level sensing with pump and valve actuation. I contributed to component procurement, wiring, system assembly, and troubleshooting, including resolving fluid leaks by changing fittings and adding sealant.
